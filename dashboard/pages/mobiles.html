Why Mobile Operators Are Essential

Mobile operators are the lifeblood of the NYQP. By driving through multiple counties, they give out rare multipliers that out-of-state stations desperately need. A single mobile can activate 10+ counties in a contest weekend, creating excitement and driving participation from hundreds of chasers. Without mobiles, many NY counties would go unrepresented on the air.

💡 Want to Go Mobile in 2026?

Plan Your Route

Target rare counties that are unlikely to have fixed stations. Northern and western NY counties are always in demand!

Equipment Tips

A simple setup works: HF radio, screwdriver antenna or hamstick, and a good logging app. N2TO used just 5W CW and 20W Phone!

County Line Trick

W9TCV operated from the ROC/ORA county line in Harriman SP - giving out two counties without moving! Smart operating.
