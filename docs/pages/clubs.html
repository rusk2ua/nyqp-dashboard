NV1Y - Boy Scouts JOTA (Jamboree On The Air)

NV1Y operated as part of the worldwide Boy Scouts of America Jamboree On The Air, introducing young scouts to amateur radio through participation in the NYQP. A fantastic example of how contest activity can engage the next generation!

Growing Club Participation

The club category welcomes all amateur radio clubs to enter the NYQP. Whether you're a large contest club or a small local group, this is a great opportunity to activate your club callsign, build camaraderie, and introduce new members to contesting. Consider entering your club in the 2026 NYQP!
